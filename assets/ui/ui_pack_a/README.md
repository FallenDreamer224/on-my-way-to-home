# Moon UI Art Pack A

23 separate PixelLab-generated transparent PNGs: 13 frames/control variants and 10 icons. The ten icons use 32 x 32 canvases. Full dimensions, visible bounds, hashes, generation IDs and prompts are recorded in `art/imports/ui_pack_a.json` and the original requests under `assets/pixellab/source/ui_pack_a/`.

Use nearest filtering, lossless imports, mipmaps off, no repeat, and native canvas dimensions. Transparent padding is intentional; do not trim or stretch these files. Keep labels in Godot Controls, separate from the artwork. This pack contains no logo, text or audio assets.

Artwork is not wired into the development theme or gameplay. Frames have differing padding and corner decoration; proposed brief margins are not verified nine-slice settings. Review content areas and state geometry before configuring StyleBoxTexture resources. The large panel is an open-center frame; small panels have filled backgrounds. Compact icons are centered with transparent padding, so their visible silhouettes occupy less than their full 32 x 32 canvases.

PixelLab usage terms: https://pixellab.ai/termsofservice . Original exports and unsuccessful candidates remain unchanged in the source archive; only selected results are copied here.
